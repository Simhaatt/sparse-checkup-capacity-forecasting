# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-08)
This is the first public release and accompanies the Journal of Energy Storage submission. It includes:
- the experiment scripts of all four stages;
- the processed data and predictions;
- the Level-1 reproduction script (`reproduce/level1.py`), which reproduces all 19 checked tables and 68 manuscript values;
- the figure sources.
